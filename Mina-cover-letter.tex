Dear Members of the Selection Committee,

I am writing to express my interest in the Education \& Training Officer position (Ref: 297\_26\_\allowbreak DIR\_\allowbreak ET\_\allowbreak ETO) within the Directors Department at BSC-CNS. Having recently completed my PhD in Intelligent Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at UPC, in collaboration with Siemens Energy, and building on a strong background in Applied Mathematics, I am keen to contribute to the team's education and training activities, particularly those connected to European initiatives such as CASTIEL3 and EuroCC3.

While my primary background is in scientific research, data science, and mathematical modeling, my experience has also consistently involved academic teaching, event organization, and project administration. I have developed a strong interest in the operational side of scientific work, particularly in the coordination and delivery of activities that bring together researchers, institutions, and participants. This position is a natural fit for this combination: my technical background allows me to understand scientific content and training needs, while my organizational experience supports the effective day-to-day delivery of programs.

My experience directly supports the practical needs of this role. In my doctoral research with Siemens Energy, I worked within a structured framework defined by work packages, milestones, and formal Deliverables. This involved preparing quarterly and semi-annual progress reports, conducting regular progress reviews, tracking project bottlenecks, and delivering presentations to international engineering teams. After completing my MSc at TMU, I worked as a Research Project Coordinator in the university’s central research department, managing administrative workflows, project evaluation templates, survey questionnaires, and milestone tracking between faculty and administration. More recently, together with two friends, I have organized community events and workshops in Barcelona, including three major events for more than 300 guests each. This venture has strengthened my practical experience in end-to-end logistics, scheduling, and stakeholder communication, while reinforcing my comfort working closely within a small, collaborative team.

In addition, teaching and educational support have been a consistent part of my academic trajectory. Through my teaching assistantships across nine courses during my undergraduate studies, and more recently through computer science course support role at ESADE, I worked directly with students and instructors, assisting with technical coursework and instructional activities. These experiences have strengthened my ability to manage recurring deadlines, respond to practical needs, and work effectively both independently and as part of a team.

Based in Sarrià neighborhood of Barcelona, I am located just minutes from the BSC and am particularly attracted to the opportunity to work on-site, as I value the direct communication, collaboration, and day-to-day interaction that are essential for coordinating activities and meetings. I also hold a permanent EU residency and work permit with no sponsorship required, and I offer immediate availability.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my expertise can directly support the education and training initiatives of your team at BSC. Thank you for your time and consideration.
